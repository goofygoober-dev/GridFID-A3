How an idea moves

Each stage has one job and one exit test. Nothing moves forward on enthusiasm alone.

Unreviewed

Found in an old repo or chat. Job: write one line on what it was and why it stalled, then sort it. Two minutes each.

Spark

A captured idea. Job: nothing yet. It waits until nothing else is in testing. Exit: you run the Idea Gauntlet on it.

Testing

Only one at a time, 14 days max. Job: prove people want it before building. Talk to 5 to 10 likely users, put up a one-page sign-up or pre-order, try a manual version for one person. Exit: two of three tests hit their number, or it gets parked or killed.

Pursuing

Earned the right to be built. Job: the smallest version that one real person uses, in 2 to 4 weeks. Exit: someone uses it or pays without you pushing them.

Shipped

Used in the real world, even if it later changed direction. Job: write what happened and what you learned. This is your track record and portfolio.

Completed

Finished and you're satisfied. No more work owed. It moves to How far you've come, with a note on what you got out of it.

Parked

Not now. Always has a reason. Star it if you truly want to come back.

Archived

Honestly done with. Hidden from the main list, never deleted, still searchable.

Killed

Tested and failed. The reason stays as a lesson.
